Industry benchmarks
NACS State of the Industry 2023 solid
1,491 combined daily transactions/store. Basket $7.80. In-store $327.6B, fuel $532.2B. Source: convenience.org
NACS / NIQ 2024 State of Convenience solid
165M daily C-store visits. Total 2024 sales $837.4B. Avg gas price $3.31/gal. Source: nielseniq.com
Convenience Store News 2024–2025 solid
In-store 38.8% of revenue, 60.7% of gross profit. Fuel 61.2% of revenue, 39.3% of gross profit. Source: csnews.com
MMCG Invest — Gas Station P&L Database 2023 solid
Median store: fuel rev ~$4.77M/yr, C-store ~$1.08M/yr, combined ~$5.86M/yr. ~320 fuel/day, ~560 in-store/day. Source: mmcginvest.com
Statista / CSNews — Weekly Transactions 2022 solid
Small operators: 3,356 in-store/week vs. 1,983 fuel/week. In-store avg $12.12; fuel avg $41.67. Source: statista.com
Fuel & traffic data
API / EIA — Gasoline Demand 2022 solid
38.9M fill-ups/day nationally. Avg 9.5–11.7 gal/fill-up. EIA 2024 avg price $3.31/gal. Source: api.org, eia.gov
LookUpAPlate — Gas Station Statistics 2025 solid
44% of fuel customers enter the C-store. Non-fuel = ~30% revenue but ~70% profit. Source: lookupaplate.com
MMCG Analytics — Traffic Capture Rate estimated
Capture rate (road traffic → pump): 0.5–3%. Derived from AADT and transaction volume cross-reference. Source: mmcganalytics.com
NACS / ITE — ADT vs Capture Rate solid
Capture rate inversely related to ADT. 10K–20K ADT: 3–5%. 20K–40K: 2–3%. 40K–80K+: 1–2%. Higher-speed, higher-volume roads reduce turn-in opportunity. Sources: convenience.org, ITE Trip Generation Manual (land use codes 945/853)
NRC Realty & Capital Advisors — Site Selection solid
Well-performing sites capture 2–3% of ADT. Corner lots at signalized intersections outperform mid-block by 20–40%. Two driveways significantly outperform one. Source: nrcrealty.com
ITE Trip Generation Manual — C-Store Traffic solid
Standard trip generation rates for gas stations (LU 945) and convenience stores (LU 853). Used to validate ADT-to-customer conversion models. Source: ite.org
Digital signage effectiveness
Herhausen, de Jong, Grewal — Journal of Marketing 2025 solid
237 campaigns, 30M shoppers. Digital signage increases purchase probability by 8.1%. Source: journals.sagepub.com
AIScreen / SeenLabs 2025 estimated
Avg 29.5–32% sales increase (industry self-reported). Menu board highlight: +15% on featured item. Source: aiscreen.io
Samsung Business Insights 2025 estimated
29.5% sales increase, 52% ad recall boost, 70% of customers influenced by digital signage. Source: insights.samsung.com
Petroleum Marketing / LED Signage Studies estimated
LED fuel price signs increase volume 10–20%. Digital message signs (EMCs) increase site traffic 10–15% in before/after studies. Sites without adequate signage see 15–25% fewer customers. Sources: petroleum marketing associations, OAAA
Customer lifetime value
Paytronix Loyalty Report 2023 solid
New loyalty member CLV: $94. By third visit CLV increases significantly. Loyalty checks 12% higher. Source: paytronix.com
NIQ / NACS Consumer Behavior 2024 solid
Avg fill-up frequency: 2.3x/month. Avg C-store shopper visits 3+ times/week. Source: nielseniq.com
Competitive economics
Hotelling — Economic Journal 1929 solid
Spatial competition model. Competitors cluster at median market position (Nash Equilibrium). Explains gas station co-location.
Maskin & Tirole 1988 / Noel 2007 solid
Edgeworth Price Cycles: sawtooth pricing in duopoly/triopoly fuel markets. Triopoly adds coordination problems. Source: peer-reviewed journals
Dallas Fed Working Paper 2025 solid
New competitor entry → 2.5¢ price drop at incumbents (~7% markup reduction). Effect immediate and persistent. Source: dallasfed.org
Assad et al. — Journal of Political Economy 2024 solid
Duopoly margins increase 28% when both stations adopt algorithmic pricing. Source: journals.uchicago.edu
Site Selection Industry Practice estimated
Right-turn-in corner advantage on homeward traffic. Corner multipliers are practitioner estimates. Sources: mmcginvest.com, kalibrate.com
Penneco Outdoor / OAAA estimated
32% purchase within a week of billboard exposure. Monument sign diversion est. 2–5%. Source: pennecooutdoor.com
Green = peer-reviewed / major trade association. Amber = industry estimate or self-reported survey.
